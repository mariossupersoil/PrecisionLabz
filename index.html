Services

What we do at your door

From a full in & out to multi-year ceramic protection. Times are estimates for a mid-size car and vary with size and condition.

  • Full in & out detail

    Ext · Int

    Hand wash, wheels and tires, door jambs, full interior vacuum, wipe-down and glass, plus a spray sealant to finish.

    Est. 2.5–3.5 hrs

  • Full interior detail

    Interior

    Steam, shampoo and hot-water extraction on seats and carpets. Leather cleaned and conditioned, salt stains lifted.

    Est. 2–3 hrs

  • Paint correction

    Exterior

    One- or two-stage machine polishing to remove swirls, haze and light scratches, after the paint is inspected panel by panel.

    Est. 2–2.5 hrs

  • Ceramic coating

    Protection

    Long-term gloss and protection that makes every wash easier. Applied after full decontamination and correction.

    Est. 5–8 hrs

  • Exterior wash

    Exterior

    Foam pre-soak, two-bucket hand wash, wheels and tires, and a streak-free dry. A quick refresh between full details.

    Est. 30–45 min

  • Headlight restoration

    Exterior

    Yellowed, foggy lenses sanded, polished and sealed against UV so you can see properly at night.

    Est. 45–60 min

  • Engine bay detail

    Exterior

    Careful degreasing and dressing of plastics and hoses. Ideal before a sale or after a long winter.

    Est. 45–60 min

  • Odour & pet hair removal

    Interior

    Pet hair pulled from carpets and seats, and smoke or food odours treated at the source rather than masked.

    Est. 1–2 hrs

The Precision Labz method

Every visit, same four steps

Every detail starts with a process. Ours is built so nothing gets missed and nothing gets redone.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    A walk-around with you: paint condition, contaminants, wear points and interior. You approve the plan before anything starts.

  2. 02

    Prep

    Decontamination, clay bar and surface correction. The foundation you can't see but will notice in the result.

  3. 03

    Detail

    Professional-grade products, applied panel by panel and seam by seam with the same attention throughout.

  4. 04

    Protect

    Ceramic coating or sealant so the finish holds up through salt, slush and summer sun.
