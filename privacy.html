What we collect

We only collect what you send us when you text, call or email: usually your name, phone number, vehicle details, location and the services you want. We use it to quote, schedule and complete your appointment and to follow up about it.

The booking form on our website doesn't send your details to us or store them. It prepares a message on your own device that you choose to send by text or email.

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing. We only share it if the law requires us to.
