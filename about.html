About us

About Precision Labz

Precision Labz is a mobile detailing service based in the Greater Toronto Area. We bring the detail bay to you: your driveway, condo garage, office lot or marina. No drop-off, no ride home, no losing a day.

Most of our work is on cars, SUVs and trucks: full in & out details, interior restoration, paint correction and ceramic coatings. We also detail private jets, turboprops and boats, where the same attention to finish matters even more.

How we work

  1. Inspect. A walk-around with you before we start. You approve the plan and the price.
  2. Prep. Decontamination, clay bar and surface correction.
  3. Detail. Professional-grade products, applied panel by panel.
  4. Protect. Ceramic coating or sealant to hold up through Ontario salt, slush and sun.

What we don't do

We focus on detailing only. We don't install paint protection film (PPF) and we don't do mechanical repairs.

Where we go

Anywhere in the GTA from Oshawa to Burlington, and west to Hamilton, the Niagara region, Kitchener-Waterloo, Guelph, Brantford, Woodstock and London.
