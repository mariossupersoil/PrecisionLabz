## Aircraft and boat detailing

- **Aviation:** exterior and interior detailing for private jets and turboprops.
- **Marine:** hull oxidation removal through full interior detailing, keeping boats dock-ready all season.

## Our process

1. **Inspect** — a walk-around with you covering paint condition, contaminants, wear points and interior. You approve the plan before anything starts.
2. **Prep** — decontamination, clay bar and surface correction.
3. **Detail** — professional-grade products applied panel by panel.
4. **Protect** — ceramic coating or sealant to hold up through salt, slush and summer sun.
